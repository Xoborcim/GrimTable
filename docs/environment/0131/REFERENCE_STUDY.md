# Composition study and original direction

References were studied for construction and composition principles. No reference
image, mesh, emblem, texture or proprietary terrain design is included in GrimTable.

- [Brokeneagle: updating a diorama base](https://modelersalliance.org/threads/updating-an-old-diorama-base-lessons-in-rubble-updated-18th-dec-completed.156226/).
  The creator reserves the vehicle area early, builds rubble at the building and
  pavement, and uses layered pigments to connect different materials. For GrimTable
  this informs reserved legal lanes, coarse-to-fine collapse fans and matching dust
  at the footing/floor boundary. These are construction principles, not a copy of
  the depicted scene or its iconography.
- [Wargameterrainblog: ruined city board](https://wargameterrainblog.wordpress.com/2016/04/13/ruined-city-board-sold/).
  The creator discusses roads, fortifications and rubble spilling across adjoining
  areas, and replacing a trench with a road to improve vehicle maneuverability.
  The lesson adopted here is that damage should cross architectural/ground
  boundaries while deliberate traversal space remains usable. No protected kit
  geometry or symbols from that board are reproduced.
- [Physical urban diorama reference search](https://jhmodelator.blogspot.com/2019/01/diorama-ciudad-alemana-segunda-guerra.html).
  Search previews informed the broad idea of incomplete street fronts enclosing
  connected ground. The source page could not be fetched; no asset was retrieved.
  A Kathy Millatt brick-debris page and an Eye of Error page were also inaccessible;
  they were not repeatedly retried or treated as implementation sources.

Original GrimTable direction: an ash-grey fortified industrial basilica with a
surviving rear arcade, half a crossing arch and collapsed west transept. Broken
footings imply the missing front elevation. A battered gate and service court sit
across an exposed crossing. Ashfall uses transport rails, severed process lines,
an abandoned press and a destroyed checkpoint instead of gothic walls.

Review criteria: a continuous building plan; source-related rubble/dust; asymmetric
mass versus usable space; visible objectives and traversable breaches. Matched
screenshots, including scenes without miniatures, are the acceptance evidence.
