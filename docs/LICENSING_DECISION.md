# Project licensing

On 2026-10-17 the project owner selected **MIT** for GrimTable.

The root [LICENSE](../LICENSE) covers original GrimTable source code,
documentation, scenarios, example packs, icons, shaders and procedural artwork,
except material with its own third-party notice. Contributors retain their
copyrights and contribute original work under MIT unless explicitly agreed
otherwise. No copyright transfer or contributor license agreement is required.

Third-party material retains its own licenses: Kenney assets are CC0-1.0,
Barlow fonts are OFL-1.1, and WebRTC components retain their MIT, MPL, BSD and
Apache/GPL alternative notices. See
[THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) for scope, source availability
and redistribution instructions.

Older milestone reports describe the licensing status at the time they were
written. This decision supersedes their statements that a license was pending.
External mod authors choose their own licenses; the loader grants no additional
rights to third-party packs.

License reference: [MIT at the Open Source Initiative](https://opensource.org/license/mit).
